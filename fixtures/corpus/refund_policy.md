# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
To be eligible, the item must be unused and in its original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
after the returned item is received and inspected. Shipping charges are not
refundable except when the return is the result of our error.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable once redeemed. Subscription
payments follow the separate cancellation terms in the billing policy.

To start a refund, open a request from the Orders page and select "Return or
refund". A prepaid return label is provided for domestic orders.
