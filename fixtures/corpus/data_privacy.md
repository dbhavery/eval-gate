# Data Privacy

We collect only the data needed to provide and improve the service: account
details, order history, and basic usage analytics. We do not sell personal data
to third parties.

You can download a copy of your data or request deletion of your account at any
time from the Privacy section of Settings. Deletion requests are completed
within 30 days, after which the data cannot be recovered.

We retain transaction records for 7 years to meet tax and accounting
obligations, even after account deletion, as permitted by law. Analytics data is
anonymized after 13 months.
