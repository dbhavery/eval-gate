# Account Security

We require passwords to be at least 12 characters long and to include a mix of
letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords are stored using salted hashing and are
never visible to our staff.

Two-factor authentication is available and strongly recommended. You can enable
it from Settings, using either an authenticator app or a hardware security key.
SMS-based codes are supported but are the least secure option.

If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and review
active sessions under Settings. Our support team will never ask for your
password or a two-factor code.
