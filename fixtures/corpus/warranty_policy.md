# Warranty Policy

All hardware products include a limited warranty covering manufacturing defects
for 24 months from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

If a product fails under warranty, we will repair or replace it at no cost. You
must provide proof of purchase and a description of the defect. Replacement units
may be refurbished but are held to the same quality standard as new units.

The warranty is transferable to a new owner for the remainder of the coverage
period. Consumable parts such as batteries are covered for 6 months.
