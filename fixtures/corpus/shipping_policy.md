# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United
States. Express shipping delivers in 1 to 2 business days for an additional fee.

Orders placed before 2 PM Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day.
Orders placed after the cutoff, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next
business day.

We ship to over 40 countries. International delivery times range from 7 to 21
business days depending on the destination and customs processing. Import duties
and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking numbers are emailed as soon as a shipment leaves our warehouse.
